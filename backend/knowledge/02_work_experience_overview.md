# Work Experience Overview

## Overview

Ankit has worked across healthcare operations, equity management, cap table workflows, developer tooling, internal platforms, and enterprise software.

His career pattern shows increasing product ownership:

- Zoho: strong frontend and product engineering foundation.
- Pulley: early startup engineer, product ownership, cross-functional execution.
- Mochi Health: led engineering and contributed to discovery for operational products in a fast-moving healthcare environment.

## Mochi Health

Role: Senior Full-Stack Engineer, Clinical Operations
Period: December 2024 to April 2026

Ankit led engineering for Clinical Operations and contributed to product discovery for workflows used by nearly 1,000 providers supporting care for 200K+ patients. He partnered directly with operational stakeholders to improve onboarding, search, credentialing, collaborating-physician workflows, and product analytics.

Strong themes:

- Improved onboarding, search, and core provider workflows while reducing manual work, errors, and support issues.
- Partnered with Insurance to build a credentialing system and migrate hundreds of thousands of records.
- Launched the Collaborating Physicians product, expanding care capacity across hundreds of providers.
- Introduced PostHog, FullStory, and Cohere to study behavior and pain points and support A/B testing.

## Pulley

Role: Senior Full-Stack Engineer  
Period: December 2020 to October 2024

Ankit joined Pulley as an early engineer and contributed across multiple product areas. Pulley was a startup environment where engineers had to own ambiguous product problems and work directly with product, design, operations, customers, and internal stakeholders.

Major product outcomes included:

- Owned the Communications Hub from concept through launch, replacing disconnected email workflows with investor updates tied to company data.
- Led frontend development of the Valuation Platform, reducing turnaround from days to about two hours and supporting 1,800+ valuations in its first year.
- Built V1 of the Offer Letter Builder as the sole engineer; thousands of offer letters were sent through it.
- Helped shape multiple 0-to-1 products with product and design from early scoping through launch and iteration.

## Zoho

Role: Software Engineer
Period: May 2017 to December 2020

At Zoho, Ankit built strong product and engineering fundamentals. He helped take a no-code website-development product from whiteboard concept to V0 while leading 4-5 engineers. He also built an interactive graph visualization library with custom layout and collision algorithms for 7,000+ nodes.

## Career pattern

Across these roles, Ankit repeatedly worked on systems where the main challenge was not only code. The challenge was understanding workflows, reducing manual work, creating reliable product behavior, and helping teams operate better.

This makes him a strong fit for product management roles where technical depth, workflow design, data-informed iteration, and cross-functional execution matter.
