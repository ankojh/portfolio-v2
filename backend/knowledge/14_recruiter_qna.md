# Recruiter Q&A

## What is Ankit's current positioning?

Ankit is positioning himself as a Product Manager with strong 0-to-1 and early-stage experience while remaining a strong fit for Product Engineer roles after 8+ years as a full-stack engineer.

He brings direct experience in discovery, user research, requirements, prioritization, product analytics, and cross-functional execution. His engineering background gives him unusual depth in technical feasibility and delivery. Do not imply that he has already held a formal Product Manager title.

## What roles is Ankit a strong fit for?

Strong fits:

- Product Manager.
- Technical Product Manager.
- AI Product Manager.
- Platform or Internal Tools Product Manager.
- Product Lead at an early-stage company.
- Product Engineer.
- Senior Product Engineer.

## What is his experience level?

Ankit has 8+ years of software engineering experience across Zoho, Pulley, and Mochi Health.

His strongest experience is owning workflow-heavy products in healthcare and fintech, with a full-stack background, strong product judgment, and repeated 0-to-1 execution.

## What companies has he worked at?

Ankit has worked at:

- Mochi Health: Senior Full-Stack Engineer, December 2024 to April 2026.
- Pulley: Senior Full-Stack Engineer.
- Zoho Corporation: Software Engineer.

## What is his strongest technical stack?

His strongest stack includes:

- React.
- TypeScript / JavaScript.
- Node.js.
- PostgreSQL.
- Next.js.
- Tailwind.
- Redux Toolkit / React Query.
- FastAPI and Python for newer AI/backend work.

## What product-management work has he done?

Examples include:

- Leading engineering and contributing to discovery for Clinical Operations workflows used by nearly 1,000 providers supporting care for 200K+ patients at Mochi Health.
- Owning a product and engineering partnership with Insurance on credentialing.
- Introducing PostHog, FullStory, and Cohere to understand behavior and pain points and support A/B testing.
- Owning Pulley's Communications Hub from concept through launch.
- Partnering with product and design on the Offer Letter Builder from scoping through launch.
- Helping shape multiple 0-to-1 products across Pulley and Zoho.

## What is his AI experience?

Ankit is developing applied-AI product depth around RAG, AI agents, LLM APIs, tool/function calling, and harness engineering.

Best phrasing:

> Ankit is a technically fluent product leader building toward applied-AI product management from a strong base in full-stack product delivery.

Avoid saying he is a machine learning researcher or AI scientist.

## Is he only a frontend engineer?

No. Ankit has strong frontend experience, but his work has included backend APIs, PostgreSQL data modeling, internal tools, operational workflows, analytics, and full-stack product ownership.

## What kind of impact has he had?

Examples:

- Built V1 of Pulley's Offer Letter Builder as sole engineer; thousands of offer letters were sent.
- Improved workflows used by nearly 1,000 providers at Mochi Health.
- Built a credentialing system while migrating hundreds of thousands of records.

## What is his education?

Ankit holds a BTech in Software Engineering from SRM Institute of Science and Technology.

## What should recruiters ask him?

Good interview questions:

- Tell me about Pulley's valuation platform.
- How did you own the Offer Letter Builder?
- How did you discover and prioritize problems for Mochi's Clinical Operations team?
- How did product analytics change a decision you made?
- How does your engineering background make you a better PM?
- How do you handle ambiguous requirements and competing stakeholders?
